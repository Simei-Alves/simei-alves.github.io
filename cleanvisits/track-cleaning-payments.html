How to track cleaning payments

A completed cleaning job and a paid cleaning job are different things. Recording them separately helps you see what is still due without relying on memory.

What to record for each visit

  • Client and date: use a reference you can recognise.
  • Agreed amount: record it before the visit and change it only when there is a new agreement.
  • Visit status: scheduled, completed or cancelled.
  • Payment received: check the cash or your account before marking it paid.

Example: completed work with money still due

Fictional amounts in euros, simply to illustrate the two states:

VisitAgreed amountWorkPayment
Client A · Monday€50CompletedReceived
Client B · Tuesday€60CompletedStill due

Completed work totals €110 in this example, but only €50 has been received. The other €60 is still due. Avoid treating all completed work as money already in your account.

Do a short end-of-day check

  1. Check which visits took place.
  2. Confirm the payments you actually received.
  3. Review the outstanding amounts and the agreed payment dates.
  4. If needed, send a polite WhatsApp reminder after checking the conversation and payment history to avoid a duplicate request.

Marked it paid by mistake? Correct the record and check that the visit appears as unpaid again. In CleanVisits you can undo a recorded payment; this changes the manual record, not an actual bank transfer.

Keep receipts and records as required for your activity. This is an organisational example and does not replace accounting or tax obligations.
