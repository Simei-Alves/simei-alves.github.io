A few things worth knowing.

Can I try it for free?

Yes. Start with 3 clients free. Additional clients can be unlocked with optional rewarded ads when available, or with Plus. Plus removes ads and unlocks all features. Check Google Play for the current local subscription price.

Do I need an account or internet?

No CleanVisits account is required. Your schedule and client records work offline on your phone. Ads, subscriptions, navigation and WhatsApp may need internet access or another app.

Does CleanVisits collect payments or send messages?

Payments are recorded manually. CleanVisits does not charge clients. WhatsApp messages are prepared for you to review and send yourself.

What languages can I use?

The app includes 20 interface languages. Choose your language when you first open it, or change it in the profile.
