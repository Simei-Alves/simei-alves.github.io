Cleaning client message templates

A short message can confirm the plan and prevent an unconfirmed journey. Use these templates as a starting point, then adjust the wording for your client.

Before you send

  1. Check the recipient and the visit date.
  2. Replace the bracketed fields and read the whole message.
  3. Ask for a reply when you need confirmation. Silence is not a new agreement.
  4. For a different request, confirm that it fits the agreed time and amount before accepting it.

Make changes clear

If you need to move a visit, identify the original appointment, propose an alternative and wait for a reply. If a client asks for extra work, record the new agreement before the visit. Avoid promising an arrival time you have not checked.

In CleanVisits: open a client or visit, choose a template, review it and open WhatsApp to send. You send the message yourself; the app does not send messages automatically.
