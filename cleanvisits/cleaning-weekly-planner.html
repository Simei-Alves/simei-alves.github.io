Free weekly cleaning work planner

A useful cleaning work schedule answers three questions: who are you visiting, at what time, and how much room is there between jobs? Use the printable template below without installing anything.

Plan your week in five steps

  1. Place your fixed appointments first. Add recurring jobs on the agreed days. Confirm those times before accepting another booking.
  2. Separate job time from travel time. Finishing one visit at noon does not make noon a realistic start for the next. Allow time for the journey and a proper break.
  3. Record what was agreed. Note the expected duration, agreed amount and supplies needed. Use a short reference for the client.
  4. Leave room for changes. Avoid filling every minute. Adjust the gap for your transport and the distance between jobs.
  5. Review tomorrow before it starts. Check the time and address the day before. Tell your client if anything changes.

Your free weekly template

Use a short reference for each client. Keep the printed sheet somewhere safe. Do not write down home access codes.

A simple example

Illustrative example: client A from 8am to 11am; travel and break from 11am to noon; client B from noon to 3pm. These are example appointments, not a prediction of how long any home takes to clean.

On your phone: CleanVisits lets you schedule repeating visits, save notes and open an address in Maps or Waze. It does not automatically calculate or optimise routes; check the journey in your navigation app.
